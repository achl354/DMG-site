## Overview

EasiLift™ is a mobile patient hoist for full-body and stand-assist lifting,
built with a wide leg-spread base to approach chairs, beds and low seating
without moving furniture first.

## Base and lifting

- Electric leg-spread adjustment, operated from the handset
- Rechargeable battery pack with a visible charge indicator
- Foot-pedal brake on all four castors

## Sling compatibility

EasiLift™ is compatible with the full EasiSling™ range, using the standard
clip and loop attachment points.
