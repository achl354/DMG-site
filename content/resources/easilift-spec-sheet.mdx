## Overview

EasiLift™ is an air-assisted floor recovery and patient lift, designed to
help teams raise patients from floor level to a safe seated or
transfer-ready height following a fall or floor-level incident.

## Lifting and inflation

- Sequential multi-chamber inflation progressively raises the patient
- Single inflation valve for controlled inflation and deflation
- Reinforced transfer handles and patient safety straps
